The Power of 10 Gbps: How Fast Internet Transforms Business

Internet speed is one of those things nobody thinks about until it's a problem — and by then, it's already cost you a client call, a deadline, or an afternoon.

What 10 Gbps actually means day to day

It means video calls that don't pixelate mid-sentence. Large file uploads that finish before your coffee gets cold. Multiple teams on the same floor streaming, backing up, and video conferencing at once without anyone noticing a slowdown.

Built in, not billed separately

Every E-District lease includes fiber internet as part of the space — no separate ISP contract, no extra line item, no waiting on an install date.

Why it matters more than people think

For content creators using our on-site studio, or teams running client demos in the presentation rooms, connection speed isn't a technical detail — it's part of how professional the whole interaction feels.
